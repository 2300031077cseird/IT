Retail BPO Transformation

Unified customer experience center for a multi-channel retailer.

The client needed to stabilize rising support volumes across phone, email, and chat while reducing cost per interaction.

Challenge

Fragmented support queues, inconsistent QA, delayed response times, and low visibility into performance.

Solution

Built a 24/7 omnichannel support desk, knowledge base, escalation matrix, QA framework, and live reporting.

Results

Faster resolution, improved CSAT, lower operating cost, and stronger workforce utilization.
